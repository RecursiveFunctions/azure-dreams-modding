Azure Dreams Shop Patcher

Barry sells two things. The monster shop apologises instead of selling anything at all. Choose what each of them stocks, name the prices, and this rewrites a handful of sectors on your disc image.

    Tick what each shop should sell. Barry takes any item or piece of equipment; the monster shop takes eggs — one for every monster in the game, not just the ones you can find in the tower. Prices are the game’s own sell values, with buying at double, until you change them.

    What it actually edits
    
      
    
        Each shop’s stock used to be a routine that spelled out its list
        byte by byte. That routine becomes a nine-instruction loop copying a
        table: Barry’s from spare room in the game’s executable, the
        monster shop’s from the apology text its buy flow no longer needs.
        Barry can list 62 goods, the monster shop 60.
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Where prices live
    
      
    
        Every item is a 20-byte record with buy and sell prices in it. Only
        the items you touch are written, and eggs are written twice, because
        the game keeps one copy for the town and one for the tower.
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Nothing leaves this page
    
      
    
        There is no server. The file is read, modified and handed back by
        JavaScript running on your own machine, and every rewritten sector gets
        fresh EDC/ECC so the image stays valid.
